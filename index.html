Submissions

What we're looking for

Full Talks & Demos (20-25 min + Q&A)

Technical talks, case studies, and practical demonstrations. Submissions are particularly encouraged in these areas:

  • Real-world deployments of Node-RED in production
  • AI integration: LLMs, agents, MCP
  • Edge computing architectures
  • Industrial use cases: PLCs, SCADA, OPC-UA, MQTT
  • Using AI tools to build & manage flows

Lightning Talks (8-10 min + Q&A)

Where the creativity of the community shines — any project you're proud of, however unconventional. IoT/IIoT and AI encouraged, not required.

Panelists

Experts with strong opinions on industrial automation, AI in industrial environments, IoT security, or the future of low-code.

Submission guidelines

  • All presentations must be in English
  • Presentations should be practical and provide real-world examples
  • Include a clear abstract (250-500 words) covering your topic, key takeaways, and intended audience
