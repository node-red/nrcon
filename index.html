Node-RED at the Edge of Intelligence

Node-RED Con is back for 2026. Last year the community showed up in force: 1,700+ registrants, 1,100+ live attendees, 35+ countries.

This year's theme is Node-RED at the Edge of Intelligence. We're looking for talks that show what's possible when Node-RED meets edge computing, industrial automation, and AI. Real deployments, hard-won lessons, and the problems worth solving.

Speaking at Node-RED Con means reaching a global audience of developers and engineers actively building with Node-RED in production. Sessions are recorded and published: last year's talks accumulated over 3,000 views on the Node-RED Con 2025 playlist.
